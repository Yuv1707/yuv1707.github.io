Career Aspirations

I aspire to work as a Software Engineer in a dynamic technology company where I can contribute to the development of innovative applications and systems. My goal is to work on projects that combine creativity, performance, and scalable architecture in real-world use cases.

Through industry research using LinkedIn, Bright Network, and Indeed, I've learned that software engineering roles require both strong technical expertise and collaborative communication skills.

My Motivation for Software Engineering

My interest in software engineering began during early programming exercises, but truly solidified as I built real-world solutions in university projects. The ability to create practical, scalable tools that help others is incredibly motivating to me. I enjoy both the creative aspects of UI and the logical structure of back-end development.

Understanding the Job Market

According to TechNation's latest UK digital economy report, software developers remain one of the most in-demand roles. Employers increasingly seek candidates with both technical fluency and communication skills, especially in agile teams. I've studied job descriptions on LinkedIn and Indeed to ensure my skills align with expectations in both junior and graduate roles.

Guest Speakers & Career Inspiration

During this module, hearing from guest speakers such as Beverley Cook reinforced the importance of professionalism and adaptability in industry roles. Their real-world experience encouraged me to consider multiple pathways into software development, including consulting and DevOps.

Career Progression Path

My goal is to begin in a junior or graduate Software Engineer role. Over time, I aim to develop into a mid-level developer, then take on technical leadership responsibilities as a senior engineer. I’m also open to hybrid roles in technical project management or product ownership.

Desired Job Characteristics

  • Role: Software Engineer
  • Location: UK-based or remote/hybrid
  • Work Type: Full-time development team member
  • Sector: Tech/startups, SaaS companies, consultancy firms
  • Culture: Agile teams, problem-solving focused, growth-oriented

Required Skills Identified

  • Technical: JavaScript, Python, Git, API integration, SQL, agile practices
  • Professional: Collaboration, adaptability, clear documentation, time management

Structured Reflection

Where do I want to be?
In a software engineering role building modern applications or platforms that make meaningful impact and offer technical growth.

What do I need to get there?
Stronger front-end and back-end experience, GitHub project contributions, and advanced coding problem-solving (e.g. LeetCode, HackerRank).

What am I doing now?
Completing coding challenges, building this portfolio on GitHub, and engaging in CPD to expand my language and framework knowledge.
