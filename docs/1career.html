Career Aspirations

I wish to be a Software Engineer in a forward-thinking technology company where I can help develop innovative applications and systems. I wish to be involved in projects combining creativity, performance, and scalable design in real-world applications.

Through industry research using LinkedIn, Bright Network, and Indeed, I've learned that software engineering roles require both strong technical expertise and collaborative communication skills.

My Motivation for Software Engineering

My interest in software engineering started early in the practice of programming, but truly accelerated when I developed actual applications in projects in university. To be able to create useful, extendable tools that help others actually excites me. I enjoy the artistic aspect of UI and the logical sequence of back-end design.

Understanding the Job Market

According to the recent UK digital economy report by TechNation, software developers remain one of the most in-demand jobs. Not only do employers want their staff to possess technical skills but also communication skills, especially if they work in an agile team. I've gone through job advertisements on Indeed and LinkedIn so that graduate and junior role expectations can be aligned.

Guest Speakers & Career Inspiration

Throughout the module, guest speaker learning reinforced the value of professionalism and versatility in career choice within industries. Their personal experience brought to mind consideration of other possible career options besides software development, e.g., consulting and DevOps.

Career Progression Path

My goal is to begin at a graduate or junior Software Engineer role. Further down the road, I hope to transition to a mid-level developer, then eventually take technical leadership responsibilities on as a senior engineer. Hybrid technical project management or product ownership are also possibilities of interest.

Desired Job Characteristics

  • Role: Software Engineer
  • Location: UK-based or remote/hybrid
  • Work Type: Full-time development team member
  • Sector: Tech/startups, SaaS companies, consultancy firms
  • Culture: Agile teams, problem-solving focused, growth-oriented

Required Skills Identified

  • Technical: JavaScript, Python, Git, API integration, SQL, agile practices
  • Professional: Collaboration, adaptability, clear documentation, time management

Structured Reflection

Where do I want to be?
In a software engineering role building modern applications or platforms that make meaningful impact and offer technical growth.

What do I need to get there?
Stronger front-end and back-end experience, GitHub project contributions and advanced coding problem-solving.

What am I doing now?
Completing coding challenges, building this portfolio on GitHub and engaging in CPD to expand my language and framework knowledge.
