Each CPD activity not only contributed to my skillset but also helped shape my personal learning habits. I found that hands-on tutorials and interactive sessions had the highest impact. This portfolio also served as a means of consolidating my learning. Writing about each experience helped me internalize the value of continuous learning and identify patterns in my growth. I now appreciate the importance of structuring learning with both goals and reflection.
