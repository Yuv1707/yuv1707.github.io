Reflection on Professional Conduct

This section reflects on my personal and professional conduct throughout the module and how it relates to wider expectations in the IT profession. It also includes reference to the British Computer Society (BCS) Code of Conduct and how I demonstrated professionalism in a learning and collaborative environment.

Engagement During the Module

I have attended all sessions punctually and participated actively in group workshops and discussions. My contributions included fulfilling rotating roles such as timekeeper and spokesperson, providing constructive input, and supporting team goals. This consistent engagement was recognised through peer feedback and workshop facilitators.

Professionalism in Collaboration

I maintained a respectful tone in all communication and upheld academic integrity across tasks and assessments. I practiced active listening, encouraged quieter members to contribute, and always approached group tasks with a problem-solving mindset. These behaviours reflect industry values such as inclusivity, responsibility, and team-first thinking.

BCS Code of Conduct Alignment

The BCS Code outlines four key principles which I aligned with during this module:

  • Public Interest: Treating all voices with respect during discussions, maintaining confidentiality, and valuing data protection during group work.
  • Professional Competence: Continuously reflecting on my own skill gaps and proactively addressing them through CPD and feedback.
  • Duty to Relevant Authority: Respecting staff instructions, submitting on time, and adhering to module expectations.
  • Professional Development: Actively participating in development opportunities, including workshops and LinkedIn Learning.

Self-Awareness and Growth

Throughout this module I have become more aware of the impact my professionalism has on others. I now recognise the importance of transparency, feedback, and resilience when working under pressure. I also feel more confident in articulating my values and professional identity.

Looking Ahead

I will continue to hold myself accountable to professional standards, especially when transitioning into workplace settings. This includes aligning to both ethical codes and cultural expectations, adapting to diverse teams, and growing into a contributor that others can rely on.
