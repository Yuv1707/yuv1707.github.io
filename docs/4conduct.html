Reflection on Professional Conduct

This section is a reflection of my own personal and professional conduct across the module and how that captures more general expectations of the IT profession. This also makes mention of the British Computer Society (BCS) Code of Conduct and how professionalism was applied with a learning environment and working within collaboration..

Engagement During the Module

I have attended sessions and actively engaged in workshop discussion and groups. My contributions were positive input, respect for other responsibilities such as timekeeper and spokesperson, and input to team objectives. Regular such contribution was appreciated by workshop facilitators as well as peer feedback..

Professionalism in Collaboration

I was considerate during all interactions and had academic integrity during tests and assignments. I applied active listening, assisted quiet members in participating, and managed group assignments with a problem-solving framework. These dispositions are a demonstration of industry values of teamwork thinking, responsibility, and inclusivity.

BCS Code of Conduct Alignment

The BCS Code outlines four key principles which I aligned with during this module:

  • Public Interest: Being considerate of all voices in discussions, maintaining confidentiality, and being respectful of data protection when working in groups.
  • Professional Competence: Regular reflection on my own skill deficit areas and actively working on them through CPD and feedback..
  • Duty to Relevant Authority: Following staff instructions, timely submission, and complying with module requirements.
  • Professional Development: Being an active participant in development exercises, including LinkedIn Learning and workshops.

Self-Awareness and Growth

I have developed a greater sensitivity to how my professionalism impacts others during this module. I now understand the importance of openness, feedback, and resilience in managing pressure. I also think that I am better able to articulate my values and professional identit.

Looking Ahead

I will uphold professional standards, especially when entering the workplace. This involves adhering to ethical codes and cultural norms, being sensitive to multicultural teams, and growing into a contributor others can rely on..
